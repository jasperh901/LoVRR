Abstract

Complex video localization and reasoning requires cross-modal semantic alignment alongside precise temporal grounding over highly sparse timelines. Existing large language models often rely on superficial one-shot visual judgments and lack deep reasoning capabilities for complex temporal tasks, leading to localization and reasoning errors.

In this paper, we propose LoVRR (Localize–Verify–Refine–Reason), a reinforcement learning-enhanced multimodal video reasoning agent. LoVRR makes two core contributions: (1) We innovatively introduce temporal reward-based reinforcement learning into a multi-agent system, endowing lightweight Chain-of-Thought (CoT) models with deliberative reasoning capabilities for precise temporal localization and inference in complex videos. (2) We propose VG-MSR (Verifier-Guided Multi-scale Search Refinement), a novel LLM-guided self-refinement algorithm that performs multi-scale re-localization under semantic grounding accuracy rewards, effectively improving localization precision and robustness while reducing computational overhead.

Extensive experiments on Grounded VideoQA, Video Temporal Grounding, and General VideoQA benchmarks demonstrate that LoVRR achieves state-of-the-art performance on complex video understanding tasks.

Method

Figure 2. Overall architecture of LoVRR. The framework consists of four roles: Planner (structured planning and query rewriting), Grounder (candidate segment generation via timestamp decoder), Refiner (zoom-in verification with binary confidence), and DeepThinker (T-GRPO enhanced deep reasoning). The VG-MSR algorithm performs iterative multi-scale refinement guided by the Refiner's confidence feedback.

LoVRR follows a progressive localize first, then think deeply paradigm. The system first narrows the temporal search space to query-relevant evidence clips, and only then applies expensive deep reasoning to refined evidence. Built upon Qwen2-VL as the backbone model, it employs a Chain-of-LoRA (CoL) role-based workflow with seamless role switching:

  • Planner: Structured planning and query rewriting; routes to grounding or direct-answering paths.
  • Grounder: Generates top-k candidate temporal segments via a timestamp decoder with spatial pooling.
  • Refiner & VG-MSR: Binary confidence scoring, candidate reranking, and iterative multi-scale boundary refinement (8 directions × 3 step sizes) with explicit budget and risk control.
  • DeepThinker (T-GRPO): RL-enhanced deep reasoning expert trained via SFT cold start followed by Temporally-aware Group Relative Policy Optimization, which rewards temporal-order dependency and suppresses shortcut reasoning.

Qualitative Example

Figure 3. Qualitative example. LoVRR progressively localizes evidence segments and applies deep reasoning. The Planner generates a structured plan, the Grounder proposes candidate segments, the Refiner iteratively refines boundaries via VG-MSR, and the DeepThinker produces a well-reasoned answer with explicit thinking process.

Key findings: With only 7B parameters, LoVRR achieves state-of-the-art results across all benchmarks. On MVBench, LoVRR reaches 67.6% average accuracy (+2.8 over VideoMind). On Video-MME Long, it achieves 53.7% (+4.5 over VideoMind). On the challenging LVBench, LoVRR achieves 40.0%, surpassing GPT-4o (30.8%). On CG-Bench, LoVRR (7B) outperforms VideoMind (7B) on all grounding metrics and even surpasses 72B–78B models on mIoU. On Charades-STA, it achieves mIoU 50.6 zero-shot, approaching fine-tuned specialists.
